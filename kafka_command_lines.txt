++> configure kafka min.insync.replicas (ISR : in sync replica) for a given topic (t-beta) with replication factor and partitions
	kafka-topics.sh --bootstrap-server localhost:9092 --topic t-beta --replication-factor 3 --partitions 3 --config min.insync.replicas=2
	
++> modify kafka min.insync.replicas (ISR : in sync replica) with replication factor and partitions for an existing topic (t-beta)
	kafka-configs.sh --bootstrap-server localhost:9092 --alter --entity-type topics --entity-name t-beta --add-config min.insync.replicas=3
